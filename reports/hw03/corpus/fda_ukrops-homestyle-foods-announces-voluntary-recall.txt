Company Announcement Date: July 30, 2026
FDA Publish Date: August 01, 2026
Product Type: Food & Beverages
Reason for Announcement: Baked products have potential for presence of aluminum slivers from the pans that were used
Company Name: Ukrops Homestyle Foods
Brand Name: Ukrops Homestyle Foods
Product Description: Baked Spaghetti and Bread Pudding products

Ukrop's Homestyle Foods, LLC, Announces Voluntary Recall of Six Products

Richmond, VA – Ukrop's Homestyle Foods, LLC today is voluntarily recalling six of its products due to the potential presence of a foreign object.

During a routine review, a small sliver of aluminum was discovered in the baking pan used to prepare six of its products. A similar metal fragment was also identified in a Chicken Cobbler product yesterday.

Out of an abundance of caution, Ukrop's is recalling all products listed below that were prepared using similar baking pans. While no injuries have been reported, consumer safety remains our highest priority.

The baking pan supplier has been notified and has initiated actions to remove all potentially affected pan lots from distribution.

Please note:

- No other products other than those listed on the chart below are impacted by this recall.
- Products were produced by Ukrop’s Kitchen from July 1 through July 30, 2026.
- All retail locations have been notified and are removing the product from the shelves.
- Consumers should check “Best By” Dates on impacted products, which range from July 6, 2026, through August 5, 2026.
- Consumers who have purchased the recalled products are advised not to consume them and should return them to the place of purchase for a full refund.
- For more information: Consumers with questions should contact Ukrop’s Homestyle Foods at 804-340-3050, Monday-Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. EST.

About Ukrop’s Homestyle Foods

Ukrop’s Homestyle Foods (UHF) began in February 2010 upon the sale of Ukrop’s Super Markets’ 27 retail locations and Joe’s Market. Retaining its kitchen and bakery manufacturing facilities and a distribution center, Bobby Ukrop’s side of the family launched the business by providing prepared foods and baked goods to its former stores (that were acquired by Martin’s which has since been sold). Led by Chairman/CEO Bobby and son-in-law and President/COO Chris Kantner, foods are delivered to hundreds of supermarkets and retail locations six days a week in the Mid-Atlantic region and beyond. Its retail customers now include: Food Lion, Harris Teeter, Kroger, Libbie Market, Publix, The Fresh Market, The Market at 25th Street, and Wegmans. In addition, UHF operates Ukrop’s Market Hall, a retail store offering the “best of” Ukrop’s products and legendary customer service. The company employs more than 500 teammates and gives 10% of its pre-tax profits back to the community as it works to nourish lives by sharing its passion for food and families. www.ukropshomestylefoods.com
