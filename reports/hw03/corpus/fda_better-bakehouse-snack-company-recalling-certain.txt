The Better Bakehouse Snack Company is Recalling Certain Lots of Donutful Chocolate Dipped Vanilla Cake Donuts to Undeclared Milk Allergen

Company Announcement Date: August 24, 2026
FDA Publish Date: August 24, 2026
Product Type: Food & Beverages Allergens
Reason for Announcement: Undeclared milk allergen
Company Name: The Better Bakehouse Snack Company
Brand Name: Donutful
Product Description: Chocolate Dipped Vanilla Cake Donuts

Las Vegas, NV – August 24, 2026 – The Better Bakehouse Snack Company is recalling certain lots of Donutful Chocolate Dipped Vanilla Cake Donuts because the product may contain undeclared milk. People who have an allergy or severe sensitivity to milk run the risk of serious or life-threatening allergic reaction if they consume this product.

Recalled products can be identified as follows: Donutful Chocolate Dipped Vanilla Cake Donuts, 10 Mini Donuts, packaged in a 7.05 oz Carton Containing 5, 1.41 oz (40g) Pouches, with UPC: 3 50041 39210 3.

Best If Used By Dates / Lot Codes:

- 9/15/2026 – Lot 26078

The recall is limited to specific lots of product manufactured and distributed in March 2026. The product was distributed to retailers nationwide and through Amazon.com.

The problem was discovered after one allergic reaction was reported in connection with the recalled product. The individual has fully recovered. The contract manufacturer has identified the cause of the mislabeling issue and has taken corrective measures.

Consumers can dispose of the affected product and obtain a refund from their point of purchase with a receipt or return the product to their point of purchase for a refund.

Consumers with questions may contact The Better Bakehouse Snack Company at 800-243-0335, Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. through 4:00 p.m., Eastern Time.

Consumer safety is The Better Bakehouse Snack Company’s #1 priority and the company maintains rigorous food safety and quality control standards. The Better Bakehouse Snack Company is working with the contract manufacturer and retailers to remove any remaining affected product from the marketplace.
